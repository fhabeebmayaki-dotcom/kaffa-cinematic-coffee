Our Story

We named it after the forest where coffee began

Kaffa is the Ethiopian highland region where coffee was first found growing wild. Our house is a tribute to that slowness — beans bought from nine small farms, roasted two streets away, and rested a full ten days before they ever meet water.

Everything here is deliberate: the low light, the heavy cups, the ninety seconds we ask you to wait while the bloom settles. It is not fast coffee. It was never meant to be.
